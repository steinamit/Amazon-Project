# Amazon Publishing Strategy

> A data-backed pitch to a CEO: which bestselling authors to back for 2025–2030, and which screen titles could become books.

## Highlights

- 550 Amazon bestsellers (2009–2019) and about 10,000 Netflix titles analyzed.
- A transparent author score weighing bestseller frequency by average rating.
- Delivered as an executive deck with KPIs and an explicit data-freshness caveat.

## Approach

Data quality checks, cleaning and de-duplication; focus on the dominant genre (non-fiction, 310 of 550 titles); top 10 authors by appearances, scored by frequency × average rating; genre and cast analysis of recent Netflix titles.

## Recommendation

Back **Gary Chapman**, the **American Psychological Association** and **Rob Elliott**, the three highest-scoring authors. Track conversion rate, repeat-customer rate and average basket value, and refresh the analysis with post-2019 data before committing.
